One broadsheet front page summing up the world's news of Saturday
21 November 2026: 36 dated stories from 7 regions, led by the COP31
deadlock in Antalya, three Houthi missiles intercepted over Riyadh, and
the magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Junin, Peru — plus a numbered world map
and the Friday market close (markets were shut on Saturday).

## How to run

```sh
python3 render.py            # re-render entry.png (needs Pillow, local only)
python3 main.py /tmp/out     # copy entry.png to /tmp/out/entry.png
```

`main.py` is standard library only (`os`, `shutil`, `sys`). It copies the
committed `entry.png` to `argv[1]`, else `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, else
`/output`, and exits 0 on success, 1 on failure. The render is
deterministic: two runs of `render.py` produce byte-identical PNGs.

## Notes on sourcing

- Every story happened on, or was first reported on, 21 November 2026.
- Where outlets disagree, the image says so: the Peru quake is given as
  magnitude 6.7 (USGS) with GFZ reporting 6.8, and the depth as a range
  (108–129 km).
- The Shenzhen trilateral itself took place on 19 November; the story
  carried here is the fallout reported on 21 November (Trump's praise,
  Zelenskyy's reaction). The 19 November ICJ Myanmar ruling was left out
  because it was neither of 21 November nor first reported then.
